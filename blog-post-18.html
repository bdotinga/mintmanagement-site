Your Website Is Sleeping on the Job.

By Brad Dotinga
November 27, 2026
5 min read

Your website is the only employee you've got who works 24 hours a day, never calls in sick, and never asks for a raise. So why does it look like it was built in 2014 and left alone ever since?

Here's the truth most owners don't want to hear: your website is already selling for you — or it's already losing jobs for you. There is no in-between. Every day, homeowners are Googling, landing on your site, and making a decision in about five seconds. And you never even knew they were there.

The 5-Second Test

Put yourself in the homeowner's shoes. Roof's leaking, or the siding's falling apart. They Google "roofing contractor near me" and click your site.

Blurry photos. No reviews anywhere on the page. A phone number buried at the bottom. Nothing that tells them what the job costs, how long it takes, or whether you've ever done one like theirs.

They hit the back button and call the next guy. That took five seconds. You just lost a job you never knew existed — and it happens every single week.

Your buddy with the ugly truck and the great crew? Same thing. The work is 13/10. The website looks like a 4/10. And the homeowner judges the work by the website, because the website is all they can see.

Your Website Has One Job

Every visitor is asking the same three questions: Can you do my job? Are you legit? How do I reach you?

If your site doesn't answer all three in seconds, it's not a website. It's a business card nailed to a tree in the woods.

Real photos of real work — your jobs, not stock photos of somebody else's house. Reviews from real customers, right where people can see them. One clear way to get a quote. That's it. That's the whole job.

And here's the part most guys miss: the leads don't come in at 10 AM on a Tuesday when you're at your desk. They come in at 9 PM. Sunday morning. While you're on a roof. A website with a real quote form catches them while you're working. A website without one doesn't.

Even your referrals check you out online before they call. Your best customer told their neighbour about you, the neighbour Googled you, and your website did the talking before you ever picked up the phone.

What a Website That Sells Looks Like

This is what we build at Mint — websites for subtrades, built by a guy who's spent 25 years as a subcontractor. I know what a homeowner needs to see before they pick up the phone, because I've been the one answering it.

Your best work, front and centre. Reviews that do the selling for you. One obvious way to get a quote. Built to show up on Google when somebody in your town searches for what you do. And built in days, not months — because the leads you're losing this week aren't coming back.

You don't need to learn web design. You don't need to write a word of it. You keep running your business, and your website finally starts doing its job — selling while you're on the roof.

The best salesman you'll ever hire works 24/7 and never calls in sick. Time to wake yours up.
